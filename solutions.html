Why All-Optical?

The Optical Advantage for AI Workloads

AI training and inference demand massive east-west bandwidth at extreme scale. All-optical interconnection removes the bottlenecks of copper-based scale-out.

  • 70% wiring-space savings versus copper, slashing rack density pressure
  • Wavelength bandwidth from 1260–1625 nm for massive DWDM capacity
  • Hollow-core fiber cuts latency further — light travels through air
  • Low-power active devices and passive structured cabling cut energy costs
  • Full ecosystem: fiber, cable, connectivity — one accountable partner
